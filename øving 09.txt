Creating Self-signed Certificates
6. What are the advantages and disadvantages of using self-signed certificates? (discuss)
    Its easy and free. You dont need the consent of a the CA and you have full control over it.
    
    But since its self signed, you cant just trust it automatically on a program level. A visitor needs to actively add it to the trust list. 
    Since its made by individuals its much more prone to making mistakes which makes it less safe. Since there is no CA, you cant know if someone is actually safe or not.
    If you want people to visit your site, good luck with that if you have a self signed certificate.

7. Imagine that you are Bob. Should you trust a self-signed certificate issued by Alice? (why/why not)
    If Alice is just some random person, probably not. You cant know if alice is trustworthy, and even if you are pretty sure they are, you cant know that youre talking to a middleman pretending to be alice. 
    A self-signed cert only proves that someone controls the private key for that cert.
    Self-signed certs are fine for private or controlled setups. If you can verify the fingerprint through a separate secure channel (and you trust that Alice is legit), youre good.

12. See https://c2sp.org/chunked-encryption: For Cobblestone, which hash function is used (full description of mode)?
    Cobblestone uses HKDF-Expand with SHA-512 for key derivation, and AES-128-GCM as the AEAD. Unless otherwise specified, SHA-512 and AES-128-GCM are recommended.
