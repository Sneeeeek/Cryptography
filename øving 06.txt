0. What are the options for combining data confidentiality and data integrity?
    Encrypt-and-MAC  -> Encrypt-and-Authenticate 
    Encrypt-then-MAC -> Encrypt-then-Authenticate 
    MAC-then-Encrypt -> Authenticate-then-Encrypt 

1. What is Encrypt-and-Authenticate?
        Encrypt your plaintext and MAC your plaintext -> Send both to the recipient. Recipient decrypts the plaintext, MACs it, and then compares them. 
    • Are there any advantages or drawbacks to authenticate (MAC) the plaintext?
        They can be parallelized.
        From the book:
            Encrypt- and-MAC is the least secure MAC and cipher composition because even a secure MAC could leak information on P, making P easier to recover. 
            This can be fixed relatively easily tho.

2. What is Encrypt-then-Authenticate?
        Encrypt the plaintext -> MAC the ciphertext -> send both to recipient. Recipient MACs the ciphertext to confirm if its correct.
    • Are there any advantages or drawbacks to authenticate (MAC) the ciphertext?
        Since you check first before doing decryption, you dont have to worry about spending effort decrypting a corrupted message.
        Since the MAC requires a key, an attacker cant fake a message easily, which makes it harder to transmit malicious data.

3. What is Authenticate-then-Encrypt?
        MAC the plaintext -> encrypt plaintext || MAC -> recipient decrypts and splits the data into plaintext || MAC to check.
    • Are there any advantages or drawbacks to encrypting the tag?
        You need to decrypt before you can check if its corrupted or not, so you can waste processing effort.
        From the book:
            it hides the plaintext’s authentication tag, thus preventing the tag from leaking information on the plaintext.

4. What is the AD in AEAD?
    AE -> Authenticated Encryption
    AD -> Associated Data

5. What kind of protection (if any) is there for the AD?
    Integrity Protection
    From the book:
        An AEAD algorithm allows you to attach cleartext data to a ciphertext in such a way that if the cleartext data is corrupted, 
        the authentication tag won’t validate, and the ciphertext won’t decrypt. 

6. AEAD: What happens if the tag is invalid?
     If the tag is invalid, the plaintext is discarded.
